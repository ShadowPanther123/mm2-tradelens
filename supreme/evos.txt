Evos are a way to get new items through earning XP!

If you buy a gamepass and it's labeled as an EVO, you get the starting knife (Variant 1). As you level up/gain EXP (with the knife equipped), the knife will evolve into a higher classed variant!

NOTE: The items are NOT tradable, can only be bought once per account, and will be deleted if you delete the gamepass from your inventory.
